Text ≥ 4.5:1 · headline scale ≥ 3:1 · button fill or outline ≥ 3:1 against its block · button label ≥ 4.5:1 on its fill.
Derive ink by measuring it against the fill — never by eye, never from a remembered table. No translucent text.
Visible focus everywhere: 2px --k-focus-ring at 2px offset.
Never meaning by colour alone — every status carries its word, every icon-only control an aria-label.
Hit target ≥ 44px on web; a dense product toolbar may go to 32px, never below. Binding contract: knowledge/COLOR-RULES.md and rules/colour.md.
