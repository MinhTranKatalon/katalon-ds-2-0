States the status of a run, case or account.

```jsx
<Badge tone="pass" dot>Passed</Badge>
<Badge tone="fail" dot>Failed</Badge>
```

Rules:
- The label text stays neutral ink on a tinted fill — meaning lives in the tint plus the dot plus the word.
- **Never a badge without its word.** A bare coloured pill fails "never meaning by colour alone".
- `pass` shares the action green on purpose: one green means "good" everywhere in Katalon.
