Identifies a person on a run, a comment or a review.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Minh Tran" size="md" />
```

Rules:
- `name` is the accessible name. Without it the avatar is decoration.
- Initials are the fallback, on the action tint. Do not substitute a stock silhouette.
- For Katalon AI use `AiStatus` / the AI mark, never an avatar.
